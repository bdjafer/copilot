SPA route change

Threads are keyed by page, and in a client-side app the page changes without a load.
  1. Comment on something in Inbox.
  2. Switch to Settings: page:change fires, the badge disappears and the panel empties. Nothing was deleted — this is a different page key.
  3. Switch back. The thread returns. The browser's Back button works too.
Why this page passes navigation.source: comment-core deliberately never patches history.pushState (SPEC §4.6.6) — in an extension's isolated world that patch would never see the page's own calls. A host that owns its router hands the engine a signal instead, which is exactly what the four lines below do. Pages that cannot do that are covered by the built-in poll (popstate, hashchange, and a URL check at the head of every anchoring batch).
Routes are ?route=… query values, so defaultPageKey keeps them and a hard reload lands on the same route.
