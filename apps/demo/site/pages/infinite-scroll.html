Infinite scroll

Two separate things are being tested here.
  • Off-screen badges. comment-ui watches each anchor with an IntersectionObserver and hides the badge when its element leaves the viewport (badge.hideOffscreen, on by default). The panel still lists the thread, so nothing is lost.
  • Late-arriving nodes. Comment on a post, scroll down until it is recycled away, then press Trim to the last 10 — the thread orphans. Press Restore and it re-anchors when its element comes back.
The engine never scans on scroll: it batches MutationObserver records, so appending a hundred posts costs one anchoring pass, not a hundred.
